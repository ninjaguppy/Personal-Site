While I am studying mathematics (and loving it), my educational roots lie in the
Liberal Arts and the Socratic Method. During my undergraduate years I had a lot
of space to write about the random (non-mathematical) ideas that were running
through my head. Now that I am in graduate school, that sort of time is hard to
come by. When I get the chance, I will occasionally post on my personal
blog---posts range from my thoughts on chalkboards to comparing Tulips with Star
Wars. If you feel so inclined, [give it a read](https://moderntiresias.com/)!
