If you want to construct 3-manifolds, there are a wealth of examples that come 
from the **mapping torus**. Given a surface \\(\Sigma\\) and any homeomorphism 
\\(f : \Sigma \to \Sigma\\), we form the mapping torus is 
\\[
    M_f = \frac{\Sigma \times [0,1]}{(p,0) \sim (f(p),1)}.
\\]
