Pinched Jordan Curves, Cannon-Thurston Maps, and their Decompositions

Joint with Steven Frankel. Link to paper

Main Theorem: Suppose that \(f \colon S^1 \to S^2\) is continuous and surjective and that there is a continuous family of maps \(f_t \colon S^1 \to S^2\), \(t \in \left[0,\infty\right]\) such that \(f_{t}\) is an embedding for \(t < \infty\) and \(f_{\infty} = f\). Then there are laminations \(\Lambda^\pm\) of \(S^1\) extending to pair of singular foliations \(\mathcal{F}^\pm\) on a closed disk \(\mathcal{D}\).

Universal Cantor Sets and Cannon-Thurston Maps for Free-by-cyclic Groups

Joint with Maxwell Plummer. Link to paper

Abstract: Given a hyperbolic free-by-cyclic group with irreducible monodromy and a component of its symmetrized BNS invariant, we construct a group action on a Cantor set that preserves a pair of laminations and that restricts to a uniform convergence group action by each associated fiber subgroup. As an application, there are only finitely many Cannon–Thurston maps for such a group that arise from fiber subgroups. We use the theory of dual laminations to \(\mathbb{R}\)-trees and Dowdall–Kapovich–Leininger’s universal real tree in an approach analogous to the zippers machinery of Calegari–Loukidou. We additionally relate certain zippers we construct to some of their zippers.

Main Theorem: Let \(G\) be the mapping torus group of a fully irreducible atoroidal outer automorphism \(\varphi\in\text{Out}(F_N)\). Let \(\Sigma_0\) be a component of the symmetrized BNS invariant \(\Sigma_s(G)\). There is a \(G\)-action on a Cantor set \(\mathfrak C\) preserving a pair of algebraic laminations and a \(G\)-equivariant map \(\mathcal R\colon \mathfrak C\to \partial_\infty G\) such that, for each rational \(u\in \Sigma_0\), there is a \(G\)-equivariant homeomorphism \(h_u\colon \partial_\infty \ker u \to \mathfrak C\) such that the Cannon–Thurston map \(i_u\colon \partial_\infty \ker u\to \partial_\infty G\) factors as \(i_u = \mathcal R\circ h_u\).
