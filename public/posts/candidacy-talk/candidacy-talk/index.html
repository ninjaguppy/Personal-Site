Wiggly Circles and Cannon-Thurston Maps

2025.07.28 Lucas Kerbs

The following is an adaptation of my candidacy talk to something readable. If you are interested in the technical side of things, there are dropdown boxes with more information.

If you want to construct 3-manifolds, there are a wealth of examples that come from the mapping torus. Given a surface \(\Sigma\) and any homeomorphism \(f : \Sigma \to \Sigma\), we form the mapping torus is \[ M_f = \frac{\Sigma \times [0,1]}{(p,0) \sim (f(p),1)}. \] The formula for this might seem a bit dry, but the picture makes the situation clear:
